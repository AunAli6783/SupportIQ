# NovaCart Refund Policy
Version: 1.8
Effective: 2026-01-01

Approved refunds are normally initiated within 5 business days after the returned item passes inspection.

Refund method:
- Card payments are refunded to the original card.
- Bank-transfer orders are refunded to the verified bank account used for the purchase.
- Cash-on-delivery orders may require bank details for electronic refund.

Partial refunds may apply when an eligible item is returned with missing accessories or packaging.

Shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless NovaCart made an error or the item arrived damaged.

If a customer believes they were charged twice, the case must be reviewed by customer support and may be escalated to the payments team.
