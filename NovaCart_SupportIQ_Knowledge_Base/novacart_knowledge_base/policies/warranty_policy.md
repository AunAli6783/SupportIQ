# NovaCart Warranty Policy
Version: 2.0
Effective: 2026-01-01

Most laptops, smartphones, and tablets sold by NovaCart include a 12-month manufacturer warranty unless the product listing states otherwise.

Covered:
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures caused by normal use

Not covered:
- Accidental damage
- Liquid damage
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Normal wear and tear

Warranty claims may require the original order number, serial number, photographs, and diagnostic information.

Extended warranty coverage applies only when explicitly purchased and shown on the customer's order.
