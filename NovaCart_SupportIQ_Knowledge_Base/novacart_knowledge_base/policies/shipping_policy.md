# NovaCart Shipping Policy
Version: 2.3
Effective: 2026-02-01

Standard delivery:
- Major cities: 2-4 business days
- Other domestic locations: 4-7 business days

Express delivery:
- Eligible locations may receive delivery within 1-2 business days.

Shipping delays:
Estimated delivery dates are not guarantees. Severe weather, carrier disruptions, public holidays, and remote-area restrictions may cause delays.

Free shipping:
Orders above PKR 50,000 qualify for standard free shipping unless a product listing specifies an exception.

Order cancellation:
Cancellation requests are normally accepted only before the order is handed to the carrier.
