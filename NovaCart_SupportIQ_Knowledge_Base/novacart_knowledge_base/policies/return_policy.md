# NovaCart Return Policy
Version: 2.1
Effective: 2026-01-01

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of receiving an eligible physical product.

Eligibility:
- Product must be unused or in resalable condition.
- Original packaging, accessories, manuals, and included items should be returned.
- Proof of purchase is required.
- Products damaged by misuse may be rejected.

Exceptions:
- Activated software licenses are non-returnable.
- Personalized products are non-returnable.
- Gift cards are non-returnable.
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" are non-returnable.

Damaged-on-arrival:
Customers should report a damaged product within 7 calendar days of delivery with photographs.

Refund timing:
After an approved return is received and inspected, NovaCart normally initiates the refund within 5 business days. The payment provider may require additional processing time.
