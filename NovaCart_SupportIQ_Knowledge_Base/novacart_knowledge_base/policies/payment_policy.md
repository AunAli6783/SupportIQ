# NovaCart Payment Policy
Version: 1.6
Effective: 2026-01-01

Supported payment methods:
- Visa and Mastercard
- Bank transfer
- Cash on delivery for eligible orders

Card verification:
NovaCart may require additional verification for unusual or high-value transactions.

Failed payments:
A failed card authorization does not necessarily mean that the order was created. Customers should verify their order status before attempting repeated payments.

Duplicate charges:
Customers reporting duplicate charges must provide the order number and transaction information. The case may be escalated to the payments team.
