# NovaCart Order Cancellation Policy
Version: 1.4
Effective: 2026-01-01

Customers may request cancellation before an order is handed to the carrier.

Once an order has been handed to the carrier, cancellation is generally unavailable. The customer may instead use the normal return process after delivery if the product is eligible.

Custom or personalized orders may enter processing immediately and may not be cancellable.

Cancellation approval is not guaranteed until NovaCart confirms it.
