# NovaCart Privacy Policy
Version: 3.0
Effective: 2026-01-01

NovaCart collects information necessary to process orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud, and improve services.

Customer support agents must not disclose another customer's personal information or order information.

The support AI must verify authorization before exposing order-specific information.

Sensitive payment credentials such as full card numbers, CVV codes, and passwords must never be requested or stored by the support assistant.
