# NovaCart Customer FAQ

## How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery normally takes 2-4 business days in major cities and 4-7 business days elsewhere.

## Can I return an item?
Eligible products can normally be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Exceptions apply.

## Is software returnable?
Activated software licenses cannot be returned.

## How long is the warranty?
Most laptops, smartphones, and tablets include a 12-month manufacturer warranty unless the product listing says otherwise.

## Can I cancel an order?
Cancellation is normally possible before the order is handed to the carrier.

## Do you provide real-time order tracking?
Yes. Order-specific status should be obtained from the order management system rather than guessed from static documents.

## Does NovaCart offer free shipping?
Orders above PKR 50,000 generally qualify for standard free shipping, subject to exceptions.

## What if my product arrives damaged?
Report the damage within 7 calendar days and provide photographs. Support will guide you through the next steps.
